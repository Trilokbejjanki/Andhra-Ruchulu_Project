👨‍🍳 About Us

Welcome to Andhra Ruchulu, your destination for authentic and delicious Andhra Pradesh cuisine. We pride ourselves on using traditional recipes passed down through generations, combined with the freshest local ingredients.

Our experienced chefs bring the true flavors of Andhra Pradesh to your table, from spicy curries to mouth-watering biryanis. Every dish is prepared with love and care to provide you with an unforgettable dining experience.
